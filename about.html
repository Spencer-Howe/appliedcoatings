Our Story
Hello Kalispell — I'm Josh.

Applied Coatings has been serving the Flathead Valley since 2017, providing high-quality bathtub refinishing and surface restoration to residential and commercial clients. We refinish bathtubs, showers, Jacuzzi tubs, garden tubs, antique clawfoot tubs, and antique farm sinks.

We take pride in delivering workmanship that transforms worn-out surfaces into restored ones — using quality materials and working with each client to find the right solution for their specific needs.

When you contact Applied Coatings, you're working directly with us — not a franchise or a call center.

What We Offer

Our Services

Professional surface refinishing for Kalispell and the entire Flathead Valley.

Bathtub Refinishing

Transform a stained, chipped, or discolored tub into a restored surface — without the expense of replacement.

  • Standard tubs, Jacuzzi & garden tubs
  • Antique clawfoot tubs
  • Porcelain, fiberglass, acrylic & cast iron
  • Chip and crack repair

Tile & Surround Reglazing

Outdated tile color dragging down your whole bathroom? We reglaze tile surrounds and shower walls with a durable new coat — no demo required.

  • Wall tile and shower surrounds
  • Grout lines sealed and refreshed
  • Color refresh or change
  • No demolition required

Sink Refinishing

Bathroom and kitchen sinks restored to like-new condition — including antique farm sinks.

  • Antique & farm sinks
  • Pedestal, drop-in & undermount
  • Porcelain and ceramic
  • Chip and stain repair

Shower Pan & Countertop Restoration

Fiberglass shower pans and countertops refinished to like-new condition for a fraction of replacement cost.

  • Fiberglass shower pans
  • Cultured marble surfaces
  • Laminate countertops

The Refinishing Process
1
Request Estimate

Fill out the contact form or give us a call. We'll review your details and follow up to schedule a free estimate.

2
Surface Prep

The surface is thoroughly cleaned, sanded, and any chips or cracks are filled and leveled before any coating is applied.

3
Coating Applied

A professional-grade refinishing coating is applied and cured to restore the surface.

4

Once cured, we walk you through care instructions before we go.
